## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Start a Project from FAQ

The page MUST include a primary call-to-action whose visible label and destination come from the content document. For this Feature the destination MUST be `/contact` and the English label MUST be “Tell us the work”. The document MUST also hold the Farsi label «کار را بگویید».

#### Scenario: CTA present

- **WHEN** a visitor views `/faq`
- **THEN** they see a “Tell us the work” control that goes to `/contact`
